List the contributions and central claims this paper makes for itself.

Rules:

- Do not editorialize or evaluate.
- Stay close to the paper's own wording; quote or closely paraphrase.
- If a claim can be located in an identifiable part of the paper, append
  that location in parentheses at the end of the bullet; otherwise omit
  the parenthetical entirely.

Output format: a Markdown bulleted list, one bullet per contribution or
claim. No heading, no preamble, no closing remarks — the list only.
